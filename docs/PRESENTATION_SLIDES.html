01. Problem Statement & Motivation

Why Classical Machine Learning Beats Generative LLMs

❌ LLM Hallucinations

Large Language Models are prone to arithmetic errors and ungrounded tax bracket estimations, risking compliance penalties.

✅ Deterministic ML

Random Forest & Gradient Boosting provide bounded, deterministic bounds with $R^2 = 0.998$ and sub-5ms inference latency.

🔒 Zero-PII Local Privacy

Parses raw CSVs locally without transmitting sensitive banking statements to third-party commercial cloud APIs.

03. Dataset Construction & Grounding

10,000 Unified Multi-Source Banking Corpus

1. HuggingFace / Agami Indian Statements

Ingested 51,945 real transactions across 200 Indian corporate bank accounts to establish real-world credit CV and salary regularity baselines.

2. Kaggle Indian UPI & Credit Card Datasets

Extracted behavioral velocity distributions from 250,000 real UPI transactions and 26,052 credit card records.

3. Indian Tech Salaries Compensation Corpus

Calibrated base salary, bonus lump-sums, and tax slab distributions across 20,960 tech compensation points.

4. Parametric Master Profile Generator

Synthesized a robust, balanced 10,000-profile training matrix with realistic noise, outliers, and marginal tax edge-cases.
